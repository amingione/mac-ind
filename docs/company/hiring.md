MAC Industrial Services is seeking an experienced Industrial Estimator to join our growing team. If you have a strong understanding of industrial construction, fabrication labor, field productivity, and project costing, we want to hear from you.

✅ Review RFQs, drawings, specifications, and bid documents
✅ Perform material takeoffs from fabrication and construction drawings
✅ Develop labor-hour estimates for:
Structural steel fabrication
Miscellaneous metals fabrication
Industrial piping fabrication
Shop welding
Field welding
Equipment installation
Plant maintenance projects
✅ Solicit pricing from vendors and subcontractors
✅ Prepare detailed project estimates, proposals, and bid packages
✅ Attend pre-bid meetings and site visits when required
✅ Work closely with project managers, shop supervisors, and field personnel to establish realistic labor productivity assumptions
✅ Track awarded projects and compare estimated versus actual costs
✅ Maintain estimating databases and labor production rates
